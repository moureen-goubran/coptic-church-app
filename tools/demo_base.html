Notes for This Demo

What's Not Working Yet, and Why

  1. The schedules are copied by hand. Everything was copied on October 21. There is no live calendar link: this page can't read other websites, and Google blocks automated reading of the St. Mark and St. George calendars. Virgin Mary & St. John's class times come from a calendar feed last updated September 1, 2025, so they may be out of date. For St. Abanoub & St. Antonious, the church's site lists no liturgy times, so the Sunday liturgy shown (8:00–10:30 AM) is an assumption. Meetings with no end time listed are assumed to last two hours.
  2. Fast and feast-season times aren't handled. During Kiahk, the Nativity Fast and Holy Week, service times change. The app keeps showing the regular weekly schedule and only reminds people to check with the church.
  3. Some buttons are limited in this viewer. The Share sheet and the Suggestion box may not work when the app is opened inside Claude. They are meant to work in a real app.
  4. Some sections are empty. Parking, church groups, church apps, Zelle or QR codes, photos, tour videos and the therapist list are waiting on information from the churches.
  5. The Arabic hasn't been reviewed by a native speaker. It is understandable, but a servant should read it before it is used for real. The new Virgin Mary & St. John class titles are the newest and least checked.
  6. Two details conflict between sources. St. Mark: the clergy phone number conflicts between sources. Virgin Mary & St. John (Bayonne): the address and phone number conflict between sources. Neither is settled until the church confirms.

Questions for the Priests

  • Who would keep each church's info updated? This is the most important question.
  • Does your church already use an app, such as ChMeetings? If so, this app would link to it instead of competing with it.
  • Where do you post everything first: the app, the website, WhatsApp or the bulletin? Could this app get access to that, through a calendar link or an export?
  • Is it OK to show your church's logo?
  • May we link your church's WhatsApp or GroupMe groups? Who should approve who joins?
  • Do you have priests who are psychiatrists, or community members who are therapists, who would agree to be listed?
  • What parking is there: a church lot (private), street parking, or a nearby public lot? Anything people should know, like permit zones, street-cleaning days or where not to park?
  • May we list priests' personal phone numbers and emails, or only the church office?
  • May we list Zelle or a QR code, and photos?
  • Which events need sign-ups, such as retreats, trips or classes?
  • What translation help do you already offer, and who would people call?

Later, If the Churches Are on Board

  • Read each church's calendar live instead of copying it. Most of them already publish one.
  • Give church admins logins so they can edit their own info.
  • Add notifications once it's a real phone app.
